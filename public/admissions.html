Admissions

Join the St. Mugaga family. Here is how the process works.

1. Check requirements

Applicants should have completed Primary Seven and have their PLE results ready.

2. Apply online

Fill in the online application form and upload a recent passport photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 2 MB).

3. Wait for review

The admissions office reviews each application and contacts the parent or guardian.

4. Report to school

Successful applicants receive an admission number and reporting instructions.
